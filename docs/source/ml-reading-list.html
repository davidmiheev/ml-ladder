Broader machine learning

Statistical foundations through geometric deep learning and ML systems — the ground beneath the post-training and safety tracks. Nine sections, filterable by format.

Anchor book · modern
Understanding Deep Learning — Simon J. D. Prince
MIT Press, 2023 — free web/PDF version at udlbook.github.io. Rigorous, notation-heavy, good fit for a math background.
Anchor book · classical
The Elements of Statistical Learning — Hastie, Tibshirani, Friedman
Springer, 2nd ed. — free PDF at hastie.su.domains. The classical-statistics grounding under everything else here.
Anchor book · probabilistic
Machine Learning: A Probabilistic Perspective — Kevin P. Murphy
MIT Press, 2012 (see also the newer two-volume "Probabilistic Machine Learning" at probml.github.io). Treats every method as inference under a probabilistic model — the throughline from Bayesian foundations to graphical models, HMMs, and Gaussian processes that the other two anchors don't cover.
